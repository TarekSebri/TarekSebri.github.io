Work Hours — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Work Hours is developed and published by Micro App Studio.

Contact: microappstudio.apps@gmail.com

1. Overview

Work Hours is designed as a privacy-friendly, offline-first application for tracking work shifts, breaks, worked time, and estimated earnings.

The core application does not require an account and does not require an internet connection.

2. Data stored by Work Hours

Work Hours may store the following information that you enter or generate while using the app:

  • job name;
  • hourly rate and currency;
  • pay-period settings;
  • shift start and end times;
  • break start and end times;
  • locally calculated worked time and estimated earnings;
  • application preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device.

3. Accounts and cloud storage

Work Hours does not require you to create an account.

The current version does not upload your core work data to a Micro App Studio server and does not provide cloud synchronization.

4. Analytics

The current version of Work Hours does not include a third-party analytics SDK.

5. Advertising

The current version of Work Hours does not include an advertising SDK.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated before that version is released to describe the advertising provider, data processing, consent mechanisms, and user privacy controls.

6. Data sharing

Micro App Studio does not receive or sell the work-history data stored locally by Work Hours.

Work Hours may use operating-system services when you explicitly perform an action such as exporting or sharing a CSV file. The destination you choose may process that file according to its own privacy practices.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your Work Hours data remains on your device unless you delete or export it.

You can delete individual work records from within the app.

Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored application data according to your device's operating-system behavior.

8. Internet access

Core features such as tracking shifts, recording breaks, viewing history, calculating estimated earnings, changing settings, and exporting work data are designed to work without an internet connection.

9. Children's privacy

Work Hours is a general productivity application and is not specifically designed or directed to children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when Work Hours changes, for example if optional analytics, advertising, backup, or synchronization features are introduced.

The updated policy will display a revised "Last updated" date.
